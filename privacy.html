Privacy.

Remagery reads design books aloud and shows each image when the text talks about it. This page says what it does with your data, in plain words.

Updated 6 November 2026

Who runs it

Remagery is a personal project by Raphael Silva, a designer in Barcelona. It's free, with no ads. Questions or requests: sayhellotosilva@gmail.com.

What Remagery can see in your Google account

  • Your email address, to know which library is yours.
  • Only the Google Drive files it makes and the PDFs you choose in Google's file picker. It can't see, change or delete anything else in your Drive.

Where your books live

  • In your own Google Drive, in a folder called Remagery: each book's text, images, cover and voice, plus a file with your progress and bookmarks.
  • On your device, in the browser's storage, so books open quickly and play offline.
  • Your PDFs are turned into books on your device. They aren't sent to Remagery.
  • Remagery has no database of its own with your books or progress.

How the voice is made

The first time a chapter is played, its text goes through Remagery's voice server (run on Cloudflare) to Google Text-to-Speech, which makes the audio. The audio comes back to your device and is saved in your Drive. The text and audio aren't kept on the server.

So everyone gets a fair share of the free voice allowance, the server keeps:

  • your email address,
  • a random code for each book you've started (not its title or text),
  • how many characters everyone has used this month.

Monthly counts are removed after about 70 days.

What Remagery doesn't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your data isn't sold or shared with anyone, and isn't used to train AI.
  • Nobody reads your books or your Drive.

Remagery's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • One book: touch and hold its cover in Remagery and remove it. Its Drive folder goes to the bin.
  • Everything in Drive: delete the Remagery folder in Google Drive.
  • On your device: remove Remagery from your Home Screen, or clear the website's data in your browser.
  • Remagery's access: remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The voice server's records: email sayhellotosilva@gmail.com and they'll be deleted.
